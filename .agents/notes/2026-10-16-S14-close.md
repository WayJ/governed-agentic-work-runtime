## Lessons

- **Byte pins with an escalation rule work**: the strongest possible D82
  evidence is an empty diff plus live hash assertions, and giving the worker
  an explicit ask-first rule (rather than a prohibition) kept the sprint
  honest when the S10 pins conflicted.
- **Design probes that prove a negative are scope gold**: "the machinery is
  already family-agnostic" turned a feared-large slice into a two-file
  change; grounding before designing paid for the whole pipeline.
- **Skip-count changes need explanations, not just records**: the 3→2 skip
  delta (conformance test activated by the opt-in) was surfaced, explained
  and independently verified — count drift without a cause would have
  poisoned every later comparison.
