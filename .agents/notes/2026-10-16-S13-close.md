## Lessons

- **Ask-before-strand paid off again**: RV-F1's counter-probe forced the
  refusal-channel amendment at design time; the implemented D74 channel was
  then independently proven cancellable end-to-end. The design→review→
  ratify pipeline is four-for-four.
- **Failed worker-start leaves nameable residue**: an `agent_readiness`
  failure still creates a worktree/branch pair; the receipt's
  release-then-fresh-restart path works, but the retry takes a `-2` name —
  verify the actual worktree your worker reports from, not the name you
  asked for.
- **Branch-checked-out-in-primary is a real integration blocker**: freeing
  main by detaching the primary is the clean option (a); record the
  coordination in both reports.
