## Lessons

- **True merges keep the ledger readable**: S8/S10 consolidation produced
  content-in-main with SHA-outside-main branches that read as "unmerged" to
  any SHA-level audit; S11's ordinary merge carries the whole lineage and the
  teardown could proceed without archaeology. Keep ordinary `--no-ff` merges.
- **Runtime restarts orphan terminal records**: `worker-release` then
  deterministically returns `release_unknown`; the receipt's recovery text
  (inspect → one fresh retry) plus a live-terminal-list check is sufficient —
  never substitute `terminal close`.
- **Heartbeat cadence is cheap coordination**: the W2/final/integration waves
  each ran ~30–60 min with heartbeats only; the coordinator stayed free and
  no state was lost across the session handoff.
