## Lessons

- **Teardown ordering caused the interruption**: the prior session executed worktree teardown
  before the close-out writes, push and CI existed, and removed its own execution carrier and
  terminal, so publication evidence never landed and the task had to be retried. Lesson now
  codified in the recovery note: a dispatched session must NEVER remove its own execution
  worktree/session before `worker_done`; teardown is strictly after the merge AND after all
  close-out writes, and applies only to the sprint carrier — never the session's own
  worktree.
- **Publication recovery without re-merging**: verify what already landed (merge parents,
  product-tree byte-identity against `591ff50`, acceptance verdict in place) instead of
  redoing completed verification, then complete only the missing acts (ledger flip, closing
  note, push, CI at the final SHA). This kept the recovery scoped and preserved the
  acceptance evidence exactly as the original run produced it.
- The product-level lessons of this sprint — first-render state flag set before the first
  render, subprocess isolation for import-purity proofs, fixture-controlled static-asset
  expectations under assembly, raw-text probes over token-rendering serializers — are
  recorded verbatim in the acceptance record's obstacles and the integration report
  (`.agents/notes/2026-10-14-S5-integration.md`).
