## Lessons

- A silent `measure … return` in an audit tool is a false-pass factory:
  every required measurement must fail loudly when its element never
  appears, in every phase — the F1 pattern (record-only skip) is worth
  grepping for in any future harness.
- Committed screenshot matrices that scroll a detail region into view
  cannot prove the page header; a small supplementary top-of-page capture
  (same CDP machinery, scrollY 0) closes that gap cheaply and became the
  standard evidence shape this sprint.
- Harness image reads that map local PNGs to a CDN cache can serve stale
  content under shared basenames; unique evidence basenames plus local
  stdlib full-pixel decoding (theme tokens present, old-theme tokens at
  zero) are the reproducible authenticity proof.
- With no workflow-file delta, the plain HTTPS push path works even for
  multi-MB image payloads; the S6 SSH fallback remains ready only for
  workflow-touching pushes.
