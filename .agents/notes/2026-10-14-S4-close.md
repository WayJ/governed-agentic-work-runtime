## Lessons

- Independent early review paid for itself twice: the file-adapter review's F1 (mutation
  surviving because `UnicodeEncodeError` subclasses `ValueError`) and the harness entry-point
  overclaim (console `publish-demo` untested) were both caught before merge and corrected by
  the original owners with reproduced RED evidence.
- Branch tips move while reviews run; merging named tips (not "the branch") and disclosing
  drift at merge time (as with the authoring audit addendum `86d4a9e`) kept the record honest
  without re-review cost.
- Probe construction is review-surface too: `json.dumps` renders float-inf as the `Infinity`
  token, silently converting an intended overflow-literal probe into a token-path retest —
  raw-text probes are the reliable shape for loader-boundary claims.
