## Lessons

- Mid-flight merge discipline: a coordinator WAIT redirect landed after a first merge pass had run;
  the recoverable pattern — reset the carrier to the pre-merge tip, verify the corrected tips'
  RED independently, hold on the sanctioned `check --wait`, re-merge only on the explicit GO —
  kept the final history a single clean merge set with no orphaned interim merges.
- Master's three review catches (present-`None` descriptor vs absent attribute, `object.__new__`
  incomplete instance, cyclic-data `RecursionError` vs `ValueError`) were all input-class gaps in
  self-review; explicit presence-vs-value and construction-bypass probe matrices are now pinned as
  tests (`DescriptorPresenceTests`, cyclic/deep-nesting ingress tests).
- D10's acceptance-before-merge sequencing (record before merge; push/remote equality evidence in
  the close-out) avoided S1's circular "acceptance requires the push that requires acceptance".
